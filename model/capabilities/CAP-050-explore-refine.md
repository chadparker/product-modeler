# Explore and refine the Product Model

The user explores the product through a capability hierarchy with dependency links and an adjacent clarification conversation.

## Important behavior

- The capability map is the primary overview.
- Selecting an entity reveals its definition, relationships, evidence, and unresolved questions.
- Branches can collapse to preserve readability.
- Changes resulting from conversation are highlighted.
- Manual correction remains possible.
