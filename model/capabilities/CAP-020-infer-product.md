# Infer product intent and behavior

The system constructs a useful provisional Product Model from incomplete Product Evidence.

## Includes

- infer the Product Frame;
- identify the Core Capability and Supporting Capabilities;
- reconstruct Journeys;
- identify Domain Concepts, rules, and transitions;
- identify Interfaces and important states;
- discover shared Subsystems and dependencies;
- retain evidence and uncertainty for consequential conclusions.

The system produces a provisional model before exhaustive clarification.
